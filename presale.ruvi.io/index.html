🚀 How to Buy RUVI

Step 1: Set Up Your Wallet

Make sure you have a non-custodial wallet like MetaMask with ETH for gas fees, and optionally USDT or USDC to buy RUVI.

Step 2: Select Your Currency

Choose between ETH, USDT, or USDC as your payment currency from the dropdown in the presale section.

Step 3: Enter Amount

Input the amount of the selected currency you want to use to purchase RUVI.

Step 4: Click Buy

Click the buy button and approve the transaction in your wallet.

Step 5: Approve (If using USDT/USDC)

You'll first approve the token spend, then confirm the actual purchase transaction.

Step 6: Import the Token

Copy the RUVI token contract address and add it as a custom token in your wallet to view your balance.
